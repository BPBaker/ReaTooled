v3.0 - November 03, 2026

HIGHLIGHTS
 * Installation: ReaTooled is now a ReaPack repository. One ReaBoot link installs REAPER, ReaPack, every extension and script ReaTooled uses, and the ReaTooled configuration and themes, and keeps them up to date through ReaPack
 * Installation: New "ReaTooled Starter" extension starts "Set up ReaTooled" automatically the first time REAPER opens, so there's nothing to import
 * Installation: New "Set up ReaTooled" merges ReaTooled into your existing REAPER instead of replacing it. Your current settings are backed up first, you're asked about any shortcuts that clash with your own, and everything else you've customized is kept
 * Installation: New "Restore settings from a backup" puts back any earlier backup, either completely or while keeping the changes you made after setting up ReaTooled
 * Theme: New default theme "Default_7.0 ReaTooled v3 Dark", plus an updated "ReaTooled v3 Light" (the v2 Light theme is still included)
 * Clipboard Manager: New panel of saved clipboard states. Copy items, keep working, then click an earlier entry to put it back on REAPER's clipboard and paste as usual
 * Smart Nudge: Nudging ("," and ".") now moves whatever you're working on: items, razor areas, envelope points or the edit cursor
 * Linking: Overhauled "Link Timeline and Edit Selection" (loop points, razor areas and the edit cursor) to work much more like Pro Tools
 * Shuffle mode: Like Pro Tools' Shuffle. Dragging an item with the Move Tool (or the Multi tool's lower half) reorders it: what came after it closes up, and it goes in at the item edge nearest where you drop it. The item moves whole, as you see it: its crossfades with its neighbors are cut where it begins and ends, and new joins get a short crossfade with auto-crossfade on (butt joints with it off). Grouped tracks and selections shuffle together
 * Trim Tool: Rebuilt from scratch to trim like Pro Tools: click anywhere in an item to trim its nearest edge, the audio stays in place, and groups, selections, ripple and butt joints all follow
 * Updating: New "Update ReaTooled" action updates ReaTooled in one step, and once a day ReaTooled tells you when a new release is out and asks whether to update now. Your own shortcuts, toolbar and menu changes and settings are kept, your window layout and screensets are left alone, and everything is backed up first
 * Grid and Nudge: The Grid and Nudge Settings can now sit inside REAPER's Transport bar, like Pro Tools' Edit window grid and nudge displays
 ^ Upgrading from v2? Your old setup is backed up before anything changes, and the Restore script can undo it at any time.
